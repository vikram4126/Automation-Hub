Welcome everyone. Today, I'm excited to walk you through our new Enterprise Internal Portal, the Automation Hub. The main goal of this platform is to submit, track, and develop automation ideas, while clearly measuring the time we save and the ROI we generate. Let’s break down each menu option.

First is the Dashboard, our main landing page. When you log in, it gives you a quick snapshot through our Key Performance Indicators. You'll see 'My Open Requests', which shows how many of your submitted automation requests are currently pending, so you don't have to dig through lists to check your ongoing tasks. 'Ideas Submitted' tracks the number of innovative ideas you've posted on the Innovation Board to encourage creative thinking. Lastly, 'My Time Saved' calculates the actual hours saved when an automation is completed for you. For example, if a daily manual task is automated, it calculates the time saved for our financial year running from October to September. We included this to show the real impact and ROI of automation, motivating everyone when they see their saved time.

Next is 'Submit Request'. This is where you describe the manual process you want to automate. Along with the description, the form asks two simple questions: How many hours does this task take you today? And how many hours do you expect it to take after automation? For example, if a task currently takes 5 hours and after automation it will only take 30 minutes, the system automatically calculates the net saving as 4.5 hours per occurrence. It then multiplies this by the frequency you select. If that task happens monthly, the system shows you 4.5 hours saved per month, and 54 hours saved for the full financial year from October to September. This real-time ROI estimate appears instantly on screen so both the submitter and the team can immediately see the business value before any development even begins. Additionally, the form includes an intelligent AI Copilot that scans your description in real-time, detects duplicate projects, and suggests the best technology and development timeline.

Moving on to 'Browse Requests' or the 'Automation Pipeline'. This acts as our visual project management pipeline with five stages: Open, Picked, Development, Testing, and Completed. It gives developers and the team a transparent view of where every task stands. Developers can come here to pick up Open tasks. Crucially, we support 'Co-Ownership' on tasks—this means multiple developers, like a designer and a coder, can be assigned to the exact same project to collaborate. The moment a task is picked up, the system automatically triggers an email notification to the person who submitted the idea, letting them know that work has officially started.

The 'My Requests' section is a detailed list showing the real-time status of all the requests you have created, complete with a progress bar. This ensures complete transparency, so you never have to chase a developer for a status update.

For our technical team, we have 'My Picked Tasks'. This screen is specifically for developers to manage the tasks they have picked from the pipeline. Here, they can update their progress from 0 to 100 percent and add notes, giving them a dedicated space to manage their active workload and easily mark tasks as complete. Once a task is marked as 100 percent complete, another automated email is instantly sent to the requester, notifying them that their new automation is ready to use.

Then we have the 'Innovation Board', which is essentially our social platform for ideas. Anyone can post a new automation idea, and others can upvote it. This helps us crowd-source the best ideas, allowing management to prioritize the ones with the most votes for future development.

To keep things engaging, the 'Leaderboard' displays a ranking of our developers and contributors. And here's the best part: we don't just reward the developers who build the tools. We also reward the employees who submit the ideas! When you submit an automation idea, you immediately earn points. When a developer finishes building your idea, you get a completion bonus. This gamification strategy builds healthy competition, encourages everyone to report manual processes, and motivates our developers to keep creating.

For the management team, the 'Reports' section features a real-time analytics dashboard built directly into the app using native Power Apps charts. It tracks the Total Hours Saved across the entire company for our October to September financial year, along with all active projects, providing leadership with a high-level overview of our company-wide ROI without needing any external reporting tools. Additionally, we have included an 'Export to Excel' feature. If a leader wants to drill down into the raw data, they can simply click this button, and an automated background flow instantly generates a detailed CSV report and emails it directly to their inbox for further analysis.

Finally, the 'Knowledge Base and Admin Panel'. The Knowledge Base is a centralized hub for training videos, articles, and guides so the team can learn self-service automation. The Admin Panel is a backend space where admins can adjust the AI Copilot's rules, keywords, and tech suggestions without needing to write any code.

In short, the Automation Hub is designed for everyone: employees can submit and track ideas, developers can pick up tasks and earn points, and leadership can monitor the overall time and money saved across our October to September financial year. Thank you!
